# Compliance and privacy

## Hunt for
- personal data where it should not be: logs, URLs, analytics, error reports, third parties
- contacting people without recorded consent, without an opt-out, or outside allowed hours
- consent that is assumed, pre-checked, or not stored with when and how it was given
- data kept longer than needed, or with no way to delete it on request
- a regulation that applies to this domain and was not considered (when unsure which, raise it as a research question instead of guessing the rule)

## Not this lens
- generic security bugs with no personal-data or legal angle (security)

## Note
Regulations change and differ by country. Never state a legal rule from memory as fact: cite a source, or flag it for research.
